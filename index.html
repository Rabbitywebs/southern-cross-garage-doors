Broken spring

The most common garage door breakdown in Perth. Springs do the heavy lifting, and heat plus daily use wears them out. If you heard a loud bang and the door suddenly feels very heavy, it's almost always a spring.

  • Loud bang from the garage, then the door won't lift
  • Door feels very heavy or only opens a few centimetres
  • Motor runs but the door doesn't move

Snapped cable

Lift cables wear and rust, especially near the coast where salty air speeds up corrosion. A snapped cable can leave the door hanging crooked, which is a safety risk, so keep clear and don't use it.

  • Door hanging lower on one side
  • Frayed or loose cable at the bottom corner
  • Door jams halfway

Noisy or stuck door

Grinding, squeaking or jerky movement is an early warning. Dust, heat and coastal air dry out and wear parts. A service now costs far less than a breakdown later.

  • Grinding, squeaking or banging
  • Door shudders or moves unevenly
  • Hasn't been serviced in over a year

Common questions

How much does a garage door repair cost in Perth?

Most repairs fall within the ranges in our price list. A service starts from $XXX and a spring replacement from $XXX. We always give you a fixed price to approve before we start.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No call-out fee within the Perth metro area. You only pay for the work you approve.

How quickly can you get to me?

We offer same-day service for most of Perth when you call before midday. If your door is stuck open and your home isn't secure, tell us and we'll prioritise you.

Why do garage door springs and cables break so often in Perth?

Springs wear out with use. Every open and close counts, and busy households get through them faster. Perth's summer heat and the salty air in coastal suburbs speed up wear and rust on springs and cables. A yearly service picks this up early.

Can I replace a garage door spring myself?

We strongly recommend you don't. Springs hold a lot of tension and can cause serious injury if released incorrectly. Call a qualified technician.

Should I repair or replace my garage door?

Most doors can be repaired. If repairs would cost close to the price of a new door, or the door is very old or damaged, we'll show you both options honestly and let you decide.

Which brands do you work on?

All major brands, including B&D, Merlin, Steel-Line, Gliderol, ATA and Centurion. If you're not sure what you have, send us a photo.
